# What the scraper image installs, named rather than filtered.
#
# Subtracting a few lines from requirements.txt left opencv-python, yt-dlp, numpy and
# Pillow in an image that opens none of them — over 100 MB for nothing. This list was
# derived from what backend/archive and backend/storage actually import.
#
# Deliberately absent: flask and psycopg (the scraper is not the API), and playwright
# (27 of the 32 brands need only plain HTTP; the 4 that want a browser are the 4 that
# produce nothing today). Adding the browser lane back means a second, larger image.
#
# curl_cffi is here for the opposite reason. It is browser-grade *access* — libcurl over
# BoringSSL with real Chrome cipher ordering — without a browser: a ~5 MB wheel and no
# Chromium, so the small image keeps its shape while the brands that refuse our Python
# TLS fingerprint become reachable.

httpx>=0.27
curl_cffi>=0.7
pydantic>=2.5
PyYAML>=6.0
beautifulsoup4>=4.12.0
lxml>=4.9.0
boto3>=1.34
anthropic>=0.25.0
python-dotenv>=1.0.0
